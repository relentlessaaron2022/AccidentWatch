Contest Rules

  1. Eligibility: The contest is open to all registered users of AccidentWatch.
  2. Submission Period: Entries can be submitted from the 1st to the 25th of each month.
  3. Voting Period: Voting is open from the 1st to the last day of each month.
  4. Entry Limit: Each user may submit up to 5 entries per month.
  5. Content Requirements: All submissions must be original photos taken by the submitter. Photos must be related to car accidents and must not contain graphic content.
  6. Moderation: All submissions are reviewed by our moderation team before being published.
  7. Voting: Each registered user may vote for multiple entries, but only once per entry.
  8. Winners: Winners are determined by the number of votes received by the end of the month.
  9. Prizes: Prizes are awarded to the top three entries with the most votes.
  10. Notification: Winners will be notified via email within 3 business days after the contest ends.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are winners determined?
Winners are determined solely by the number of votes received by the end of the month. The three entries with the most votes win first, second, and third place respectively.
When will I receive my prize if I win?
Prizes are distributed within 7 business days after the winners are announced. Amazon Gift Cards are sent to the email address associated with your account.
Can I submit photos from accidents I didn't witness personally?
No, all submissions must be original photos taken by you. Submitting photos taken by others is against our rules and may result in disqualification.
Can I edit or enhance my photos before submission?
Minor adjustments such as cropping, straightening, and basic color correction are allowed. However, significant alterations that change the factual representation of the accident are not permitted.
What types of accidents are eligible for the contest?
All types of vehicle accidents are eligible, including car, truck, motorcycle, and bicycle accidents. The focus should be on the accident scene, vehicle damage, or environmental factors contributing to the accident.
