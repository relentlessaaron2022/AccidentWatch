Safety Tips

  • Avoid Distracted Driving

    Never text and drive. Keep your attention on the road at all times.

  • Always Wear Seatbelts

    Seatbelts reduce the risk of fatal injury by 45% for front-seat occupants.

  • Keep Emergency Kit

    Have a first aid kit, flashlight, and emergency contact information in your vehicle.
